💡 Judge Presentation Defense & Physics Explanation

Q: Why is the mixer tank volume 3,150 mL?
"Our mixer tank collected 3,150 mL, representing an actual paint delivery error of only +1.67%. Visual observation confirms ~100 mL of clear water was discharged first. This was stagnant water held in the 70 cm hoses from prior water tests. Incoming paint displaced this water plug into the tank like a fluid piston. Net paint was 3,050 mL (+50 mL due to motor coast-down), and Mustard colour was confirmed!"
Q: Why is K_flush (1.723) different from K_blue (1.034)?
"Both utilize Line 3 (Pump 3 & FT-03), but water is 15× less viscous than paint (~1 cP vs ~15 cP). Water experiences virtually zero viscous boundary layer drag against the rotor blades, so it spins the turbine faster per unit volume. In software, we decoupled Line 3 into dedicated paint (K=1.034) and flush water (K=1.723) slots, achieving 99.90% accuracy (1,998 mL)."
